About

Last Flag is a fast-paced 5v5 third-person hero shooter from Night Street Games—a studio co-founded by Imagine Dragons lead vocalist Dan Reynolds and his brother Mac Reynolds. Teams hide their flag, hunt the enemy’s, and defend a capture to win matches framed as a wild 1970s televised game show. Built in Unreal Engine for PC (Steam and Epic Games Store).

Responsibilities & Contributions
  • Lead audio design for Night Street Games: creative direction, priorities, and quality bar across marketing and in-game milestones.
  • Senior sound design for heroes, abilities, weapons, UI, and match-critical feedback in a fast 5v5 capture-the-flag format.
  • Technical sound design: implementation, systems, optimization, and stem delivery for cinematics, trailers, and gameplay.
  • Trailer and marketing sound-to-picture: sound design, music, and loudness passes for beats shared with the public.
  • Close collaboration with directors, design, and engineering on readability, iteration, and ship schedules.
