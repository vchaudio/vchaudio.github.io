About

Large-scale live celebration shows produced by MARIN ART's for national events in Tajikistan. The opening act “History” tells the country’s story through staged scenes, choreography, and spectacle—composed and produced end-to-end for arena-scale playback, including Navruz (2024) and the 30th Independence Anniversary show (2021).
